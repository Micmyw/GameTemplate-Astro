Obstacle Orbit uses a circular track instead of a finish-line course. The ball keeps moving, and the challenge is deciding when to swap between the inner and outer rings as barriers rotate through each lane.

## How to play

Watch a complete barrier cycle before making a risky switch. The safest opening is not always the first one you see because the destination lane may already contain the next hazard. Surviving several rotations gradually increases the course speed.

## Control approach

Use one deliberate press for each lane change and wait for the move to finish. On touch screens, tapping near the center prevents accidental browser-edge gestures. This entry remains a draft and exists only to verify that unpublished content never reaches public routes.
