Roll Ball 3D turns each course into a sequence of route choices. One lane may be shorter but unstable, while the safer branch asks you to manage several small landings without building much speed.

## How to play

Look for the lane that gives the next platform a straight entry. Tilting bridges keep their previous movement for a moment, so begin correcting before the ball reaches the edge. Checkpoints divide the course into compact challenges and make experimentation less costly.

## Control approach

Drag or steer in measured bursts, then watch how quickly the ball responds. A brief reverse input can reduce sideways drift before a landing. Touch controls work best in landscape orientation, but tight split paths may require more precision than a small screen provides.
