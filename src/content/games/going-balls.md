Going Balls is about preserving momentum without letting speed make every decision for you. The route rises above open space, so a small correction made too late can turn into a long fall.

## How to play

Read the next two obstacles before accelerating. Wide platforms are useful places to settle the ball, while narrow bridges reward short steering inputs instead of holding a direction continuously. Moving gates follow a repeatable rhythm, so waiting half a second can be faster than restarting a run.

## Control approach

Use alternating taps to keep the ball near the center line. Before a ramp, straighten your approach and let momentum carry the ball upward. On mobile, keep a finger near the middle of the control area so both left and right corrections remain comfortable.
